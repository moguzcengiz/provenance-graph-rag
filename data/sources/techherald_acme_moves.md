# Acme AI relocates its headquarters to Munich

*Tech Herald - Business desk - 15 June 2025*

The document-AI start-up Acme AI moved its headquarters from Berlin to Munich
this month, citing closer ties to industrial customers in Bavaria.

"Munich gives us direct access to manufacturing partners," the company said.
Bob Chen is the CEO of Acme AI. After a recent hiring round, Acme AI has about
180 employees.

Munich is located in Germany.
